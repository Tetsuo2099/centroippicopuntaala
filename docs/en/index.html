Polo Club

Punta Ala has long been a historic venue for major international and national equestrian competitions in polo and show jumping. With a unique heritage, enhanced by the extraordinary beauty of the Tuscan hills and their lush landscapes, Punta Ala is recognised internationally as one of Italy’s most exclusive destinations.

In the Seventies and Eighties, the Punta Ala ground hosted thrilling polo matches featuring many of the leading figures of this fascinating sport.

CIP

Welcome to Centro Ippico Punta Ala (CIP), your trusted riding school in Tuscany. Set in picturesque Punta Ala, in the heart of the Maremma, our equestrian centre offers a unique riding experience on both horses and ponies.

At CIP we offer riding lessons for all levels – beginners, intermediate, advanced and competitive riders.

Our group and private lessons are taught by qualified federation instructors in show jumping. We welcome riders from the age of 4, in a safe and stimulating environment to learn and grow.

At Centro Ippico Punta Ala you can also take a horse on full or half loan, building a deeper bond with your horse.
Discover the beauty of riding in Tuscany with Centro Ippico Punta Ala.

We look forward to welcoming you!

Our facilities

  • Arenas: two sand arenas of 50×100 m and 35×70 m, a sand round pen 16 metres in diameter and a 270×140 m grass field.
  • Stables and paddocks: 31 brick boxes and 30 wooden boxes, plus 19 paddocks of various sizes.
  • Wash areas: indoor and outdoor, with hot water.
  • Training track: 400 metres long.
  • Tack rooms: 6 well-equipped tack rooms.

Michaela Ricci

International-level Swedish rider and instructor.
Michaela Ricci is a Swedish rider and instructor who competes in dressage at international Grand Prix level.
She has a solid reputation in the equestrian world, having brought many horses to national and international competitions up to Grand Prix level. In 2008 she moved to Florence, Italy.
Her ability to train horses from the very first breaking-in up to Grand Prix level rests on a methodical approach: gradual training, a tailored method, patience and perseverance.
Michaela puts her technical skills into her teaching, focusing on harmony between horse and rider – the key to success in dressage!

Fox hunting

(simulated)

Fox hunting on horseback, born in the 18th century, is an old British tradition that today is mostly practised in simulated form.

No wild animal is harmed: the “fox” is a rider wearing a fake tail, which the pursuers must snatch from his green coat.

It is a social and sporting event that recreates the chase with full respect for nature and animals.
