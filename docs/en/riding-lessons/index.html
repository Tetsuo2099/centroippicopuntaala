Riding

Set in beautiful Punta Ala, on the Tuscan coast, Centro Ippico Punta Ala (CIP) offers a wide range of opportunities to learn and refine your riding skills.

Our Riding School welcomes riders of every level:

Beginners, Intermediate, Advanced and Competitive riders.

We offer group and private show jumping lessons, taught by qualified federation instructors. We welcome riders from the age of 4, and you can also take a horse on full or half loan.

In August, polo lessons are also available.

Open to riders from the age of 8, they are tailored to the individual needs of each student.

Lessons are private or in small groups of up to 2 riders, for personal attention and intensive learning.
