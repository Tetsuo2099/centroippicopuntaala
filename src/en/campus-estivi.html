Summer Camps

Centro Ippico Punta Ala (CIP) is delighted to offer its Summer Camps, a unique experience for children aged 5 to 8. With a maximum of 6–8 children per camp, we make sure every child gets the individual attention they need to learn and have fun.

Our camps run in the morning, from 9am to 12pm. During this time, children get to know the ponies, learn grooming and care techniques, and take part in introductory pony games.

Every day also includes a snack and arts and crafts to spark the children’s creativity.
During the summer camp, children also meet the vet and the farrier, learning how important the care and well-being of horses is.

Discover the excitement and adventure of our Summer Camps at Centro Ippico Punta Ala. We can’t wait to welcome your children!
