Horse Livery

Located in Punta Ala, in the stunning Tuscan Maremma, Centro Ippico Punta Ala (CIP) offers a high-quality horse livery service.

Our English-style livery provides stabling in boxes or paddocks, ensuring maximum comfort for your horses.

We offer private lessons with federation instructors, and our instructor can also work your horses for you.

This ensures your horses receive the attention and care they need to thrive.

From October to April, horses can access the beach (50 m from the stables) and enjoy the benefits of a walk in the sea.

We also offer a range of additional services on request, including:

  • Breaking-in and training
  • Farrier
  • Veterinary service
  • Osteopathy
  • Clipping
  • Laundry
