Riding Clinics

At Centro Ippico Punta Ala (CIP) we are proud to offer Riding Clinics for those who want to develop their equestrian skills further.

We organise clinics in show jumping, dressage and vaulting, led by experienced and qualified instructors.

Our clinics are designed to provide intensive, focused training, allowing riders of different levels to refine their skills and technique in a stimulating and supportive learning environment.
