Simulated fox hunting

The Società Toscana Caccia alla Volpe (Tuscan Fox Hunting Society) was founded in 1993 by Colonel Vittorio Alborino and NH Aldobrando Rossi Ciampolini.
Its founding spirit rests on good manners, mutual esteem and trust – essential qualities for admission to the Society.

Fox hunting on horseback, born in the 18th century, is an old British tradition that today is mostly practised in simulated form. No wild animal is harmed: the “fox” is a rider wearing a fake tail, which the pursuers must snatch from his green coat. It is a social and sporting event that recreates the chase with full respect for nature and animals.

The hunt is a cross-country ride along a set course of about 20–25 km, often featuring natural obstacles (logs, hedges, ditches), all of which can be avoided. Today it is an equestrian activity that celebrates the bond between horse and rider, offering the freedom of riding in open country, good company and challenging courses, in a setting that evokes tradition.

The Master leads the field of riders. The Society currently has over 40 members, plus a number of occasional guests. Hunts usually take place on land made available by large estates, where the Society prepares courses whose length and difficulty suit each event.

Hunt meets take place once a month and traditionally end with a convivial lunch, after which honours are paid to the Fox, the Master and the Hunters, with special thanks to the hosts. We would like to highlight our outstanding collaboration with Gaia Bulgari and Centro Ippico Punta Ala, a strong and fruitful partnership that has continued for several years.
