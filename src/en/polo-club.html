Punta Ala

Polo Club

Punta Ala has long been considered a historic venue for major international and national equestrian competitions in polo and show jumping. With a unique heritage, enhanced by the extraordinary beauty of the Tuscan hills and their lush landscapes, Punta Ala is recognised internationally as one of Italy’s most exclusive destinations.

In the Seventies and Eighties, the Punta Ala ground hosted thrilling polo matches featuring many of the leading figures of this fascinating sport.

After a 30-year break, the Punta Ala ground is shining in a new light and looks to the future with ambitious goals. All this has been made possible by the careful and passionate restoration led by Gaia Bulgari, president of the Punta Ala Polo Cup organising committee.

“Our goal, ever since we began renovating the facility in 2019, has been to restore it to its former glory,” says Gaia Bulgari.

Summer season 2026

The 2026 Summer Polo Season in Punta Ala awaits you with days of pure sport, elegance and adrenaline. Between the charm of the sea, the warmth of the sun and the thrill of the field, enthusiasts and champions come together to celebrate polo in a unique and unforgettable atmosphere.

Location

The Punta Ala Polo Club, with stables of 51 permanent boxes and room for several hundred temporary boxes, features a regulation polo field of 380×140 metres and a sand track 350 metres long.

Next to the field are the stands, seating up to 400 people, just 50 metres from the beautiful beaches of the Tuscan coast.

The facility also includes two outdoor sand arenas, one of 30×70 metres and the other of 50×100 metres, plus a sand round pen 16 metres in diameter. Expansion plans are already under way, including a 6-horse walker.
