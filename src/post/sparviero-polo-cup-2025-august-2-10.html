Summer Season 2025
Opens at Punta Ala

Cría del Mar Wins the Opening Tournament

The SUMMER SEASON 2025 officially begins at the Punta Ala Polo Club, marking the start of a new polo season on the Tuscan coast.

Just steps from the sea, where the ocean breeze meets the untamed beauty of the Maremma, the opening tournament sets the tone for a summer dedicated to sport, competition, and elegance in a truly unique setting.

After a series of intense and well-balanced matches, Cría del Mar claims victory in the opening tournament, standing out for teamwork, consistency, and quality of play. The final delivers strong rhythm and competitive spirit, confirming the high technical level of the season’s first event.

With two unmissable tournaments scheduled throughout the summer, Punta Ala Polo Club once again proves to be a landmark for polo in Tuscany, where sport, nature, and lifestyle come together on the coast.
