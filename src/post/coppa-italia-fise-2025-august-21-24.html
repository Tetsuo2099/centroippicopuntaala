Summer Season 2025 Opens at Punta Ala

Cría del Mar Wins the Coppa Italia FISE 2025

A great achievement for Cría del Mar, crowned champion of the Coppa Italia FISE 2025 – US Polo Assn. Trophy.

The tournament delivers high-level competition, confirming itself as one of the most prestigious events in the national polo calendar. Throughout the competition, Cría del Mar stands out for consistency, teamwork, and technical quality, culminating in a decisive performance that secures the title.

Winning the Coppa Italia FISE 2025 represents a significant milestone, rewarding dedication, preparation, and sporting spirit, and further reinforcing the team’s presence at the highest level of Italian polo.

A well-deserved victory in a competition that celebrates excellence, tradition, and passion for the sport.
